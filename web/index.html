    Score against the baseline

    Better / worse / within noise, counted per result, from the compared side's point of view. sysbench is left out of the count when the architectures differ.

    Metric by metric

    Each cell is the difference from the baseline: green means that side is better (for latency, lower is better), red worse, grey within noise (at most 1% or the run-to-run variation (CV) of either side, the rule of vmbench report compare). Hatched cells are sysbench across architectures: shown, not counted. Choose a metric name to see every value.
